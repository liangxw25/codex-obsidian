# 云小满 Look Mechanics

Yun Xiaoman is a chibi Chinese-style little girl pet. Direction should read through:

- pupil and eyelid placement inside the large eyes
- small head turn or head tilt
- face/nose/forehead huadian orientation
- slight scroll angle changes
- twin braid sway that supports the head direction

Do not rotate the whole sprite to fake direction. Keep the same body scale, red-white cloud dress, silver cloud crown, forehead huadian, twin braids, and weather scroll. Cardinals must be unmistakable:

- 000 up: pupils/head look upward, crown remains upright
- 090 screen-right: face and pupils turn to viewer's right
- 180 down: pupils/head look downward toward the scroll
- 270 screen-left: face and pupils turn to viewer's left
